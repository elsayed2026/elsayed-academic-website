About me

Research-led teaching for a changing world

I am a university lecturer at the Faculty of Languages and Communication, Universiti Pendidikan Sultan Idris (UPSI), Malaysia. My academic interests focus on Arabic language teaching and learning for non-native speakers, with particular attention to learner-centred approaches and the responsible use of digital tools.

My work explores ways to support language development through meaningful classroom activities, AI-assisted learning, and technology-enhanced instruction.
